Crypto Tax-Loss Harvesting Explained

Tax-loss harvesting is a legal strategy where you sell investments that have fallen in value to "realise" a loss, which can then offset your taxable gains. Because crypto is volatile, it often presents opportunities to do this.

How it works

Capital losses generally offset capital gains of the same type first, and many countries let you carry unused losses forward to future years. By realising a loss in a year when you also have gains, you reduce your net taxable gain — and therefore your tax bill.

A simple example

Suppose you have a $10,000 gain from selling one coin and another coin is currently sitting at a $4,000 unrealised loss. If you sell the losing position, your net taxable gain drops from $10,000 to $6,000.

Watch out for wash-sale rules

In some countries, "wash-sale" or "bed and breakfasting" rules disallow a loss if you rebuy the same asset within a set window (for example, 30 days). The treatment of crypto under these rules varies and has been changing, so check the current position in your jurisdiction before repurchasing.

Step-by-step approach

  1. Review your portfolio for positions trading below their cost basis.
  2. Estimate your realised gains for the year.
  3. Consider realising losses to offset those gains.
  4. Confirm any wash-sale timing rules that apply to you.
  5. Keep clear records of every sale and repurchase.
