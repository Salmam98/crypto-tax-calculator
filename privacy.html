Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 June 2026

The short version

CryptoTaxBit is designed to protect your privacy. The crypto tax calculator runs entirely in your web browser. The figures you type into the calculator are processed on your own device and are never sent to, collected by, or stored on our servers.

Information we do not collect

We do not require an account and we do not ask for your name, email, wallet addresses, or transaction history. Your tax inputs are not transmitted anywhere.

Analytics and cookies

We may use privacy-respecting, aggregate analytics to understand overall site usage (such as page views). Any such analytics do not include the figures you enter into the calculator. If we use cookies, they are limited to essential or anonymous analytics purposes.

Third-party links

Our guides may link to external resources such as official tax-authority websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, please reach out via the contact details published on our site.
