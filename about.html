About CryptoTaxBit

CryptoTaxBit is a free, privacy-first tool that helps cryptocurrency investors quickly estimate their potential tax liability on capital gains and crypto income. Our goal is to make crypto taxation easier to understand, whether you are filing for the first time or simply planning ahead.

What we do

Our calculator runs entirely in your browser. We never collect, store, or transmit the figures you enter — there is no account, no tracking of your numbers, and no data sent to a server. Alongside the calculator, we publish plain-English guides explaining how crypto is taxed in different countries.

Our approach to accuracy

Tax rules change every year and vary by country and personal circumstance. We base our educational content on published guidance from tax authorities such as the IRS (United States), HMRC (United Kingdom), the CRA (Canada), and the ATO (Australia). The calculator provides a simplified estimate to help with planning and is not a replacement for professional advice or dedicated filing software.

Why trust us

  • Transparent: our content cites official tax-authority guidance.
  • Private: your figures stay on your device.
  • Free: no paywall, no sign-up, no upsell.

Important disclaimer

CryptoTaxBit provides general educational information and estimates only. It does not constitute tax, legal, or financial advice. Always consult a qualified tax professional before filing your return.
